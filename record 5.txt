2. Find the number of customers in each city, and only include cities with more than 2 customers

mysql> SELECT city,count(*) as number_of_customers from customer group by city having count(*)>2;
+---------+---------------------+
| city    | number_of_customers |
+---------+---------------------+
| Calicut |                   4 |
+---------+---------------------+
